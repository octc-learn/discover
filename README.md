# Discover OCTC

**Explore. Connect. Grow.**

[Visit Discover OCTC](https://octc-learn.github.io/discover/)

## About

Discover OCTC is a student engagement hub for campus events, on-demand activities, Merit opportunities, and timely student information at Owensboro Community & Technical College.

## Design philosophy

High-tech behind the scenes. Analog-inspired on the surface.

- Student-centered, accessible, mobile-friendly, and visually engaging.
- Authentic campus imagery and event flyers.
- Date-driven content and simple ongoing maintenance.

## Features

- This Week at OCTC — featured event carousel
- Discover In Person and Discover On Demand
- Merit opportunities
- Upcoming events calendar
- Important student notices
- Captioned mini-video link support
- Discover Rewind: a curated academic-year collection of approved campus photos and video highlights

## Status

Prototype v0.4 in development. The Rewind collection is ready for approved campus media; no photos or memories have been invented.

## Content maintenance

Current events and activities: `data/discover.json`. Yearbook collections: `archives/index.json`. See `AGENTS.md` and `archives/2026-2027/README.md` for media approval and accessibility requirements. Keep the same permanent site URL from year to year.
